# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 21, 2026**

## Overview

PolarUniversal Global Compliance OS is a decentralized application (dApp) that operates on public blockchain networks. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our platform.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or process any personal data.**

Our dApp operates entirely on-chain and does not maintain any centralized databases containing user information.

### What We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- IP addresses or location data
- Browsing history or usage analytics
- Cookies or tracking identifiers
- Financial account information

### Blockchain Interactions

When you interact with our smart contracts, the following information is publicly recorded on the blockchain by design:

- Your wallet address (pseudonymous identifier)
- Transaction hashes and timestamps
- Smart contract interaction data

This data is inherent to blockchain technology and is publicly visible to anyone. We do not control or have the ability to modify blockchain data once recorded.

## Third-Party Services

### Wallet Providers

We integrate with third-party wallet providers (e.g., MetaMask). These services have their own privacy policies which govern their collection and use of data.

### RPC Providers

Blockchain queries may be routed through third-party RPC providers. Please review their respective privacy policies for information about their data practices.

## Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no centralized data to secure. Your private keys and wallet credentials are never transmitted to or stored by our application.

## Your Rights

As we do not collect personal data, traditional data subject rights (access, deletion, portability) do not apply. Your blockchain interactions are controlled by your private keys.

## Children's Privacy

Our dApp is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
