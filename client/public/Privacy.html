Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 21, 2026

PolarUniversal is a decentralized application built on the Solana Mobile Stack. We value your privacy and operate under the following principles:

  • No Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Non-Custodial: We do not have access to your private keys or your funds. All transactions are signed locally on your device.
  • On-Chain Data: Only your public wallet address and transaction history are visible on the public Solana blockchain.
